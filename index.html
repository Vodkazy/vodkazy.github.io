Biography

Currently I am an Algorithm Expert on the Intelligent Sales & Service team of the 1688 department at Alibaba Group, where I lead a team to build the Agentic Service for B2B sales operations in the AI era. Pairing agentic capabilities with a new form of product design, we are redefining what an intelligent sales system for business customers should look like, reshaping the operating model of sales, and ultimately lifting the commercialization efficiency of B2B selling. Before that, I earned my Master’s degree in Computer Science and Technology from Nanjing University in 2022, where I worked on Knowledge Graphs (KGs), Relation Linking and Knowledge Graph Question Answering (KGQA) under the guidance of Professor Wei Hu in the Websoft Research Group.

Recently, my research interests mainly focus on Large Language Models (LLMs), Agent Applications (Memory) and Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI). I am exploring how reinforcement learning on the base model, together with a well-designed Agent Memory framework, can strengthen an agent framework’s ability to handle vertical-domain tasks, so that AI can solve real business problems in industry and move a step closer to AGI.

Selected Publications

EMNLP 2026 Oral
SPAR: Experience-Driven Strategy Evolution in Multi-Session B2B Sales
Hangming Zhang, Yao Zhao†(Co-author), Haotian Kuang, C. L. Philip Chen, Tong Zhang, Tengyue Ma
B2B outbound sales require multi-round relationship-building, but existing LLM agents lack cross-session memory and strategy refinement. We propose SPAR, a self-evolving framework that models selling strategies as testable hypotheses and refines them via a double-loop architecture with episodic memory and causal attribution.
ACL 2025
Making RALM Robust to Irrelevant Contexts via Layer Knowledge Guided Attention
Weijie Shi, Hao Chen, Jiaming Li, Yao Zhao†(Co-author), Yazhong Zhang, Qijin Chen, Jipeng Zhang, Ruiyuan Zhang, Jia Zhu, Jiajie Xu, Xiaofang Zhou
Retrieval-augmented language models (RALMs) alleviate LLMs’ factual hallucination and knowledge obsolescence, but irrelevant retrieved passages impair performance. We propose a layer-knowledge guided attention method that leverages LLMs’ layer-wise knowledge to assess passage relevance and enable selective fusion, outperforming existing approaches on RALM benchmarks.

EMNLP 2025
DIDS: Domain Impact-aware Data Sampling for Large Language Model Training
Weijie Shi, Jipeng Zhang, Yaguang Wu, Jingzhi Fang, Shibo Zhang, Yao Zhao†, Hao Chen, Ruiyuan Zhang, Yue Cui, Jia Zhu, Sirui Han, Jiajie Xu, Xiaofang Zhou
We propose DIDS, a domain impact-aware sampling method for multi-domain LLM training. It uses gradient clustering (with proxy models/dimensionality reduction) to ensure intra-domain consistency and a FIM-guided metric to quantify domain impact. By combining impact assessment, loss trajectories and marginal return effects, DIDS achieves 3.4% higher average performance with comparable training efficiency.

ACL 2022
Implicit Relation Linking for Question Answering over Knowledge Graph
Yao Zhao†, Jiacheng Huang, Wei Hu, Qijin Chen, Xiaoxia Qiu, Chengfu Huo, Weijun Ren
Relation linking (RL), a key module in KBQA, maps natural language (NL) relations to knowledge graph (KG) relations, but struggles with implicit NL relations and KG incompleteness. We propose ImRL, which links NL relation phrases to KG relation paths via a path ranking model that aligns textual and structural embeddings, plus a gated attention mechanism incorporating external paraphrase knowledge. Experiments on multiple datasets demonstrate ImRL’s superiority over state-of-the-art methods, especially for implicit RL.

WWW 2022
Trustworthy Knowledge Graph Completion Based on Multi-Sourced Noisy Data
Jiacheng Huang, Yao Zhao†, Wei Hu, Zhen Ning, Qijin Chen, Xiaoxia Qiu, Chengfu Huo, Weijun Ren
We propose a trustworthy method for open knowledge graph (KG) completion that mitigates web data noise. It combines a holistic-scoring graph neural network, value alignment networks, and a source-quality-aware truth inference model with semi-supervised learning, outperforming state-of-the-art methods in both missing fact completion and new fact discovery.

APWeb 2019
Leveraging Context Information for Joint Entity and Relation Linking
Yao Zhao†, Zhuoming Xu, Wei Hu
We propose EEARL, an extended entity-relation linker for KBQA systems, which leverages full context (including non-mention expressions like wh-words ignored by existing methods) via character- and word-level LSTMs to extract mention context and entity/relation attribute features, then re-scores candidates by vector similarity. Experiments on LC-QuAD and QALD show EEARL outperforms comparative approaches in both entity and relation linking accuracy.
