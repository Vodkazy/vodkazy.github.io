Short Biography

Currently I am a Senior Algorithm Engineer at Alibaba Group’s 1688 Business Unit, working with the innovative 88Cha team to drive intelligent solutions. I earned my Master’s degree in Computer Science and Technology from Nanjing University in 2022, conducting research under the guidance of Associate Professor Hu Wei in the Websoft Research Group.

Research Interests

My research interests lie at the intersection of Knowledge Graph Question Answering (KGQA), Entity Linking, Relation Linking and large language models (LLMs). Currently, I am primarily focused on AI-Search System, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and the evaluation of LLMs.
